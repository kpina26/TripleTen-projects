
# Taxi Services in Chicago

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Chicago taxi trip data to uncover key trends in ride demand, trip durations, and the impact of external factors such as weather. 
The analysis identifies the top-performing taxi companies, the most popular drop-off locations, and investigates whether weather conditions 
significantly influence trip durations on Saturdays.

---

## Results & Insights
- **Market Share:** Flash Cab dominates the market with the highest ride count, while competitors handle significantly fewer trips.
- **Popular Drop-off Locations:** Loop and River North show the highest demand, indicating concentrated activity in commercial/business districts.
- **Weather Impact:** A statistical t-test confirmed that bad weather significantly increases trip durations on Saturdays.

---

## Conclusion & Recommendations
- Focus on optimizing operations for peak-demand areas like Loop and River North.
- Implement weather-based service adjustments to improve efficiency during adverse conditions.
- Leverage demand insights for targeted marketing and improved driver allocation.
